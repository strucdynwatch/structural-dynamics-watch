# About

Structural Dynamics Watch collects papers, PDB statistics and news on dynamic and time-resolved crystallography in one place. It updates automatically every night.

Data sources: PubMed (NCBI), bioRxiv and the RCSB Protein Data Bank. Automatic searches miss things and sometimes include things they shouldn't; suggestions and corrections are very welcome.
